01 / Strategic framework

How Tesla competes

Use these marketing concepts to interpret the company's approach. This is an educational analysis, not an official statement from Tesla.

PRODUCT

Electric vehicles, software-enabled features, charging access, and an ecosystem of related services.

PRICE

Examine how pricing, product versions, incentives, and perceived value shape customer decisions.

PLACE

Consider direct sales channels, online purchasing, showrooms, delivery, and charging infrastructure.

PROMOTION

Analyze earned media, social platforms, product announcements, referrals, and public brand visibility.

POSITIONING

Explore the association of Tesla with electric mobility, technology, innovation, and performance.

PERSONAL BRANDING

Elon Musk's public profile can increase attention while also creating reputation risks for Tesla.

02 / Strategic review

SWOT analysis

STRENGTHS

Recognizable brand; experience in electric vehicles; software and charging ecosystem.

WEAKNESSES

Brand reputation exposure; dependence on a focused product portfolio; customer experience challenges to monitor.

OPPORTUNITIES

EV adoption; energy storage; software and services; expansion into new customer segments.

THREATS

Growing EV competition; changing regulations; price pressure; reputational controversy.

These are discussion points for a classroom case study. Validate and refine them with current evidence before presenting them as factual findings.
